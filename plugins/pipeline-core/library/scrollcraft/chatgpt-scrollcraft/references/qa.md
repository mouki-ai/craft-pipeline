## Content

- The first screen establishes product, audience, and reason to continue.
- Each act has one job and one dominant message.
- The CTA is singular, specific, and reachable from the close.
- No invented proof, filler section, or unexplained interaction remains.

## Motion

- Scroll always produces a meaningful visual or narrative change.
- Adjacent acts do not use the same motion family.
- The peak is visible, readable, and has enough scroll room.
- Reduced motion preserves sequence and meaning.
- Media has a poster or static fallback and does not require audio.

## Responsive

- No horizontal overflow at 320–390px widths.
- Headings do not collide with media or controls.
- Sticky/pinned scenes release cleanly.
- Tap targets are comfortable and hover is not required.
- The close and CTA are visible without a precision interaction.

## Accessibility

- Heading order is logical and landmarks are present.
- Images have useful alt text; decorative images are hidden from assistive tech.
- Focus is visible and follows reading order.
- Contrast is checked on the actual composited background.
- Motion can be reduced without losing content.

## Handoff

- State the exact preview/deploy URL, if one exists.
- List changed files and unresolved issues.
- State which viewports and modes were actually checked.
- Do not call a screenshot, code check, or local preview a production launch.
